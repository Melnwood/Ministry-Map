How young people are moving toward Christ — across the movement.

Every youth group that uses Ministry Map contributes anonymous zone counts. Together they answer the questions no single group can: where does the journey slow down, and where is God moving?

🔒 This dashboard sees zones, never names. No student identities, no individual tracking, no leader notes — heart-notes never leave the leader's own coach. Only counts and durations are aggregated here.
🧪 Showing demo data — connects to live aggregates automatically once groups are on the deployed app.

How long does each step of the journey take?

Movement this semester

How this is computed without tracking anyone: each group's app saves check-ins as anonymous position rows. The aggregation runs server-side: it measures, per student record, the time between first appearing in one zone and first appearing in the next — then throws the identities away and reports only counts and medians. A country appears once it has 3+ groups, so no group is identifiable.
